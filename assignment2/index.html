Australia LGBTQ Mental Health Index

Mental health issues are a huge issue in Australia right now since at least 20% of residents suffer from them. This can be due to many reasons such as social media, isolation, and a decline in the economy. We can also see that it is an especially severe problem among young people and this might be due to social pressure related to work, housing, relationships, and social media. Between 2017 and 2018, 30% of female Australian residents suffered from mental health related issues and 21.3% of male Australian residents between the age of 15 to 24 suffered from mental health related issues.

While mental health issues are big issues regardless of group, they are significantly more severe in the LGBTQ+ community. This can be due to social stigma, work discrimination, discrimination from family and friends, financial instability, and loneliness. LGBTQ+ people are much more likely to experience violence, bullying, and being unemployed due to their sexuality and gender identity. All of those factors contribute to mental health issues such as anxiety, depression, and suicidal thoughts which significantly worsens the overall mental health of the LGBTQ+ community. Visualisations show how transgender people are much more likely to experience mental disorders, suicidal thoughts, and self harm behavior than cisgender people and how gay, lesbian, bisexual, and different terms of people are much more likely to experience mental disorder, suicidal thoughts and self harm behavior than heterosexual people.

The graph on the left displays various strategies that Australian LGBTQ+ individuals choose to self-manage their mental health issues. 58% of bisexual people choose to seek support from family or friends, and 51.3% of gay or lesbian people choose to increase their level of exercise or physical activities. To decrease the amount of LGBTQ+ individuals suffering from mental disorders or mental health-related issues, we need to focus on building those environments for people. If you have any LGBTQ+ family members or friends, they will greatly appreciate your effort if you try to support them or participate in exercises with them to enlighten them.
